# MyRugbyDiary Product Direction

## Vision
MyRugbyDiary is a rugby coaching and athlete-management platform designed to help coaches plan training, monitor players, and manage team operations in one place. The product should centralize weekly planning, player performance, accountability, and match preparation without requiring a disconnected spreadsheet workflow.

## Core product themes
- Coaching operations
- Athlete development
- Performance tracking
- Team communication and coordination
- Match and training planning

## Current product position
The application already contains the foundation for:
- coach authentication and role-based access
- roster and player management
- training calendar and session planning
- fixtures management
- performance metric tracking

The next major product phase is to turn this into a complete weekly training design system with individualized athlete prescription.

## Strategic direction
The product should evolve in this order:
1. Coach planning tools
2. Athlete-specific training prescription
3. Performance monitoring and progression
4. Recovery and readiness insights
5. Team communication and reporting

## Product principle
The coach should be able to design a program once and assign athlete-specific execution based on each player’s training goal, rather than recreating separate plans for every athlete.

### 1. Training operations

### 2. Player management

- Position assignment

- Hypertrophy programming

- Weekly load planning

### 4. Performance analytics
- Metric comparisons against targets

### 5. Team communication
- Player communication logs
- Availability and injury updates
- Fixture coordination
- Training reminders

## Product goal for the next milestone
The next milestone is to implement a Training Goal system that allows each player to be assigned one of the following:
- Strength
- Hypertrophy
- Endurance

Once selected, each athlete receives the appropriate rep scheme for exercises when the coach programs them.

This keeps the training system consistent while making the prescription athlete-specific.
